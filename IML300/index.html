Project 1

Interactive Audio & Visual with p5.js

Open live sketch

This is a music player with built-in music and track switching through left and right arrows. It combines background music and natural sounds, and the structure is arranged so new music can be added by changing the preload section and registering a new sound class in the array.

Project 2

HyperNarrative

3 project ideas Pre-exercise Travel World With Whale

My first complete webpage. For the theme of fun, I made it colorful and added playful GIFs. The travel-world version lets the whale move you through different places with clicks, using music and cute visual language to create a relaxed experience.

Project 3

Networked Care

Enter Project 3

This project was built around the idea of secrets and encouragement. It lets users store and retrieve messages, creating a simple system for reading whispered words from others and sharing your own.
